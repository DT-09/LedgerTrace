# Caseweft — Commercial Operating Thesis

## Decision

Do not compete as another AP automation suite. That category already includes invoice capture, 2/3-way matching, approvals, payment execution and ERP integrations from established vendors. Caseweft cannot credibly win by claiming to automate more of the invoice lifecycle.

The narrow position is **vendor-neutral exception evidence review for a defined parked-invoice queue**. The buyer already has an accounting/ERP system. Caseweft is useful only when a recurring queue still requires staff to reconstruct invoice, PO, receipt and optional payment/credit evidence manually.

## Ideal customer profile

- Distributor, wholesaler or manufacturer with item-level purchasing.
- AP team with a recurring parked-invoice queue.
- Existing accounting/ERP system can export invoice, approved-PO and goods-receipt lines.
- Named AP owner or Financial Controller responsible for the backlog.
- A bounded queue where source evidence is sufficiently complete to compare.
- Buyer can approve a small paid evaluation and an authorised data-transfer process.

Disqualify organisations whose issue is only non-PO invoices, supplier-statement-to-ledger reconciliation, tax treatment, ERP integration, automated approvals or payment execution. Those are outside the current implementation.

## Why the problem exists — and what remains unproven

Public AP research and vendor materials consistently describe invoice matching and exception handling as operational work. AP automation research also shows that matching and exception workflows remain only partly automated in many organisations. This supports the existence of the workflow category; it does **not** prove that a specific company has an unmet need, that Caseweft will reduce cost, or that any buyer will pay.

The market is crowded. Established platforms advertise two- and three-way matching, configurable tolerances, approvals, supplier portals and ERP connectivity. Caseweft must therefore sell a narrower job: a portable, explainable, source-row evidence pack for a queue the customer's existing system has not resolved. If buyers say their current platform already provides this evidence at acceptable cost, disqualify the opportunity rather than invent a differentiator.

## Paid evaluation — productised, bounded

- One supplier or one defined parked-invoice queue.
- One accounting period or backlog window.
- Up to 500 invoice, PO and receipt lines per source, subject to file-fit review.
- Optional payment, credit and amendment records only where needed.
- Written scope, file-fit decision, data-handling method, fee, delivery target and acceptance criteria before kickoff.
- Deliver a prioritised exception register with source rows, comparison logic, calculations, unresolved questions and limitations.
- Buyer reviews and confirms any accounting or supplier action.
- No success fee tied to speculative recovery; no claims that flagged amounts are recovered cash.

The USD 500 figure previously discussed is an unvalidated price hypothesis. Do not publish it as established pricing. Validate willingness to pay through a real, authorised purchase decision before setting a permanent price.

## Distribution that does not depend on mass cold email

1. **Search-intent pages:** publish focused, useful pages for parked invoice review, PO/receipt mismatch evidence, partial-receipt reconciliation and AP exception workpapers. Each page must explain the supported workflow and limitations; no fabricated case studies or rankings.
2. **Self-qualification:** the public site should show a working synthetic case, required input schema, disqualifiers, local-first data boundary and a clear paid-evaluation inquiry.
3. **Referral surfaces:** make a concise partner brief for fractional controllers, bookkeeping/accounting firms and ERP implementation advisers who encounter unresolved exception queues. This is a referral hypothesis, not an existing channel or partnership.
4. **Evidence-led conversion:** collect only company/workflow metadata. Qualify whether a real queue, buyer, budget and usable exports exist before discussing confidential data.
5. **No fake traction:** no fabricated customers, logos, metrics, testimonials, recovery amounts, accuracy rates or implied endorsements.

Organic discovery is not guaranteed. Search visibility and partner referrals require time and independent validation. A website rebuild cannot itself create paid demand.

## Commercial gates

A prospect becomes qualified only when all are true:
- A named buyer owns the recurring queue.
- The queue exists now and its size/age can be measured.
- The current system does not already solve the evidence problem adequately.
- Invoice, approved PO and receipt exports contain usable join keys.
- The buyer can authorise a paid evaluation and lawful data handling.
- The buyer agrees to written acceptance criteria.

A paid customer exists only after an authorised agreement and cleared payment. An inquiry, demo, meeting, sample run or verbal compliment is not revenue.

## Product gates before recurring subscription

Do not sell recurring SaaS until verified identity, tenant isolation, persistent cloud review cycles, access control, audit history, secure transfer, retention/deletion, backups, monitoring, support, subscription entitlement, checkout and cancellation are implemented and independently tested. The current app is local-first and has none of those SaaS controls.

## Operational constraint

The public inquiry endpoint stores contact/scope metadata only. It does not accept accounting files or payments. Do not accept confidential buyer data through the form, email or messaging apps. An authorised business representative must handle contracts, payment-provider accounts and data-processing obligations where required.
