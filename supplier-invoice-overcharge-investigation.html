01 / The problem

Why this needs more than a matching rule

Finance teams often need to reconcile invoice line prices and quantities against the agreed order and what was received. Without source-level comparisons, a variance can become a slow email trail rather than a reviewable case.

LedgerTrace's role: surface candidate discrepancies, show the records behind them and make unresolved evidence visible. A candidate is not a confirmed loss, liability or recovery.

06 / Common questions

Before you start

Does a PO price difference prove an overcharge?

No. Check amendments, contract terms, taxes, freight, substitutions, unit conversions and effective dates before classifying it.

Can LedgerTrace review quantities?

Where compatible invoice, PO and receipt quantities are supplied, it can compare them and flag differences for human review.

Will it automatically dispute an invoice?

No. It produces evidence for a finance reviewer. It does not contact suppliers, block payments or post accounting entries.
