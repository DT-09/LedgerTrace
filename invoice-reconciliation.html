Buyer questions

Before you share a workflow

Does LedgerTrace automatically recover money or block payments?

No. It organizes candidate discrepancies and evidence for a human reviewer. It does not issue payment instructions or claim that a candidate is a confirmed loss or recovery.

Do you need confidential invoice data to start?

No. Start with the public illustrative sample. Initial evaluations should use synthetic or approved anonymized records. Data handling must be agreed before any sensitive records are shared.

How much does the evaluation cost?

The fee is negotiated privately based on agreed record volume, data quality, checks and review effort. Scope, timeline, fee and acceptance criteria are confirmed in writing before work begins.

What if our CSV format is different?

Column mappings and sample compatibility are assessed during scoping. A check is included only when the required inputs and expected outcomes can be defined clearly.
