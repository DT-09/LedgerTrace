01 / THE OPERATIONAL FAILURE

Exceptions age because the evidence is scattered.

A parked invoice can involve an invoice line, a PO amendment, a partial receipt, a supplier mismatch and an approval trail. The reviewer must reconstruct the case across separate exports before deciding what to do.

01 / BLOCKED

Invoice cannot be cleared

Price, quantity, supplier or missing-reference differences leave invoices waiting for investigation.

02 / FRAGMENTED

Evidence lives in separate files

Invoice, PO and receipt records come from different systems and rarely arrive as one reviewable case.

03 / UNRESOLVED

Handoffs lose the reasoning

A spreadsheet flag alone does not show which rows were compared, what rule fired or what evidence is missing.

04 / PRODUCT BOUNDARIES

Evidence first. Claims kept narrow.

Proofrail is a browser-based evidence workbench and bounded review service. It is not an ERP, payment platform or certified security product.

Browser-side CSV processingSelected accounting CSVs are read and processed in the browser tab. The app does not currently upload those CSV contents to Proofrail.
Supported matchingInvoice lines are compared with PO lines and goods-receipt quantities using supported keys and rules. File-fit limitations apply.
Extended evidenceThe recovery engine adds supported payment, supplier-credit and PO-amendment evidence. Candidates are not confirmed losses or recoveries.
Not implementedSupplier-statement-to-ledger reconciliation, ERP integrations, automatic approvals, payment execution and customer-validated accuracy claims.
