    Resolution / 02.8

    Record the outcome

    Case notes stay in this browser session until exported.

    Only record a credit, refund or correction when supported by source evidence. LedgerTrace does not independently verify bank receipts or supplier acknowledgements.

    Calculation boundary

    This CSV investigation estimates candidates using the supplied records. It does not authenticate source documents or fully model tax, freight, currency conversion, tolerance profiles, retainage, split/merged lines, remittance-level partial payments or every credit-note scenario. A reviewer must validate each case before making a supplier claim or accounting entry.

    05 / Methodology

    A finding should explain itself.

    LedgerTrace applies explicit comparisons to the records provided. It does not infer intent, decide whether an invoice is fraudulent, or replace an AP review process.

    01 / MATCH

    Connect the source rows

    Normalize identifiers and compare invoice lines with purchase-order and receipt rows using PO number and item code. Missing or ambiguous relationships must remain visible.

    02 / EXPLAIN

    Show the rule and values

    Flag configured conditions such as duplicate invoice IDs, supplier differences, price deltas, quantity excesses, missing receipts and unmatched source rows.

    03 / REVIEW

    Keep the human decision

    Inspect the evidence, record review notes and export the worklist. A finding is not a payment instruction, and opening it does not resolve it.

    Evidence standard: traceable, qualified, reviewable

    Each finding should identify the compared records and source rows, show the values and rule behind the result, and surface conflicting or missing inputs. A “match” means only that no configured rule triggered; it is not approval to pay. Payment-excess values remain candidates until a reviewer verifies them against source documents.

    06 / Product details

    Clear answers before you evaluate.

    The important details about matching, inputs, evidence and review are spelled out before you decide whether to use LedgerTrace.

    Which records can I compare?

    Three CSV exports: supplier invoice lines, purchase-order lines and goods-receipt lines. Each file should include the relevant identifiers, item code and quantity; invoice and PO files should include unit price for price checks.

    How are records matched?

    The current matching key is purchase-order number plus item code. LedgerTrace then checks configured conditions such as duplicate invoice identifiers, supplier differences, invoice-versus-PO price differences, quantity excesses, receipt gaps and unmatched PO lines. Confirm column mappings and edge cases before using a new data shape.

    What can I inspect in a finding?

    Select a result to review the invoice row, supplier-compatible PO and receipt rows, compared values, triggered rule, and explanation. Source-row references point back to the uploaded CSV line numbers. Review notes and exports support the next human decision.

    Where is uploaded data processed?

    In the current version, selected CSV files are processed in your browser; they are not uploaded to a LedgerTrace application server. Use synthetic or approved anonymized data for evaluation, and do not provide confidential records until data handling has been agreed.

    What needs separate validation?

    Currency conversion, tax treatment, complex split or merged invoice lines, and full supplier master-data resolution are not general capabilities. Currency conflicts now block amount-based recovery calculations, and receipts from a conflicting named supplier are excluded. A “matched” status means no configured rule triggered; it is not a certification that an invoice is correct or approval to pay.

    How do I know the results are useful?

    Use a human-reviewed reference set with expected outcomes. Compare findings against that set, record both missed exceptions and false positives, and agree the scope and acceptance criteria before a paid evaluation. Illustrative sample records demonstrate the workflow; they are not customer results.
