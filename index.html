05 / Method

Separate the signal from the claim.

The tool organizes evidence and calculates candidates. A finance reviewer must confirm that source records are complete, comparable and authoritative before a supplier claim is made.

01
Find candidates

Match invoice lines to supplier, PO, item and receipt records. Missing or conflicting records stay visible.

02
Reconstruct expected

Use the matched PO price and quantity supported by order and receipt records; consider supplied effective amendments and credits.

03
Compare payment

Use invoice-linked payment records. Missing payment references do not count as proof of a paid overpayment.

04
Build the case

Export the calculation, findings, source row numbers, proposed corrective action and reviewer notes.

05
Verify outcome

Track a user-entered credit, refund, correction or rejection with a supporting reference. Cash recovery is never inferred.

Current product boundary
This is a local, CSV-based investigation MVP, not an ERP integration or autonomous recovery service. It does not yet reliably resolve split/merged lines, complex invoice-level tax and freight, currency conversion, tolerance policies, partial-payment allocation by remittance advice, or every credit-note scenario. Those cases require manual review; do not use the estimate as a journal entry or a supplier demand without validation.
