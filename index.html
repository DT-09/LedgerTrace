01 / THE OPERATING GAP

The exception is not the answer. It is the beginning of the investigation.

A parked invoice may involve split receipts, a changed PO, a supplier identity conflict or a credit that was never applied. A red cell in a spreadsheet does not reconstruct that chain. The reviewer needs to know exactly what was compared—and what remains unknown.

01 / DISCONNECTED RECORDS

One case spans several exports

Invoice, purchase-order, goods-receipt, payment and credit data arrive in separate files with different identifiers and levels of detail.

02 / AMBIGUOUS MATCHES

A number is not proof

Repeated invoice references, partial deliveries, amendments and supplier conflicts can make a simple join unsafe.

03 / WEAK HANDOFFS

Flags lose their reasoning

Finance needs the source row, comparison, triggered rule, calculation and unresolved question—not just a variance total.

03 / HOW CASEWEFT WORKS

A bounded evidence workflow that fits beside the system of record.

Caseweft is not an ERP replacement or an invoice-capture suite. It is a second-pass review workbench for supported exports where finance teams need to understand and document exceptions.

THE REVIEW ENGINE

Reconstruct. Compare. Explain.

Import supported CSV exports, map their fields, run deterministic checks and inspect each candidate against its source rows.

  • Invoice-to-approved-PO and invoice-to-receipt comparisons
  • Duplicate candidates, missing references and supplier conflicts
  • Quantity, unit-price and line-arithmetic checks
  • Supported payment, supplier-credit and PO-amendment evidence
  • Currency, allocation and data-quality gates that withhold unsafe calculations

04 / COMMERCIAL FIT

Designed for a defined queue—not every finance problem.

The best fit is an AP team at a distributor, wholesaler or manufacturer that already has an accounting or ERP system, can export line-level records, and has a recurring PO-backed exception queue that still needs manual reconstruction.

Good fitParked PO-backed invoices; recurring price or quantity mismatches; split receipts; incomplete evidence; repeated manual investigation.
Required inputsInvoice lines, approved PO lines and goods-receipt lines with usable supplier, PO and item references. Optional payments, credits and amendments for separately scoped questions.
Initial evaluation boundaryOne supplier or one defined queue, one period/backlog window, up to 500 lines per source subject to file-fit review.
Commercial engagementPaid, fixed-scope evaluation. Scope, fee, delivery date, data handling and acceptance criteria are agreed in writing before any work begins. No unpaid custom implementation.

05 / EVIDENCE AND DATA BOUNDARIES

Trust comes from what the system refuses to claim.

A rule can identify a candidate. It cannot establish supplier fault, accounting treatment or recovered cash by itself. The reviewer remains accountable for the decision.

Browser-side processingSelected CSVs are parsed and processed in the browser tab. The current workbench does not upload their contents to the inquiry API.
Local cycle historyFindings and reviewer notes can be saved in passphrase-protected AES-256-GCM archives. The customer controls storage and the passphrase; a lost passphrase cannot be recovered.
Withheld calculationsAmount-based conclusions are withheld where currency, supplier identity, allocation, amendment effectivity or other required evidence is incomplete or conflicting.
Not implementedSupplier-statement-to-AP-ledger reconciliation, ERP integrations, authenticated customer accounts, tenant-isolated cloud workspaces, shared team review, subscription billing and automated accounting actions.
No unsupported outcome claimsNo customer-validated accuracy, customer references, confirmed savings, recovered cash or production security certification is claimed.
Data handling before evaluationDo not send confidential records through the public form, email or WhatsApp. An approved transfer method and written scope must be agreed before any buyer data is accepted.
