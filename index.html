    03 / Who it serves

    For finance teams that need to explain a mismatch—not just flag one.

    Designed for accounts-payable leads, controllers, and finance operations teams reviewing supplier invoices against purchase orders and goods receipts. Most relevant where spreadsheet checks are repetitive and exceptions need a clear trail back to source rows.

    A / THE PROBLEM

    Checks are scattered.

    Invoice, PO and receipt data often sit in separate exports. A status or total alone does not tell a reviewer which row disagrees or why.

    B / THE OUTPUT

    One reviewable trail.

    Compare configured fields, see the triggered rule, inspect source-row references and export exceptions for human review.

    C / THE BOUNDARY

    Assist review; do not replace it.

    This is an early-stage workbench, not an ERP, payment approval system, audit certification or promise that every exception will be detected.

    Why consider a test?

    Choose it for inspectability—not for a claim we cannot yet prove.

    • Rules and compared values are visible.
    • Sample data can be reset explicitly.
    • Results and exceptions can be exported for review.
    • Scope, acceptance criteria and fee are agreed before paid work starts.
    How a first client can find this

    A clear sample earns attention. A relevant introduction earns the meeting.

    This website is a proof point, not a lead-generation engine by itself. The realistic route is to show the working sample to AP leaders and finance operators, and build referral relationships with bookkeeping, finance-operations and ERP implementation consultants who already serve those teams.

    1. Show the evidence.Share the live sample and a short, role-specific explanation of the check it performs.

    04 / Methodology

    A finding should explain itself.

    LedgerTrace applies explicit comparisons to the records provided. It does not infer intent, decide whether an invoice is fraudulent, or replace an AP review process.

    01 / MATCH

    Connect the source rows

    Normalize identifiers and compare invoice lines with purchase-order and receipt rows using PO number and item code. Missing or ambiguous relationships must remain visible.

    02 / EXPLAIN

    Show the rule and values

    Flag configured conditions such as duplicate invoice IDs, supplier differences, price deltas, quantity excesses, missing receipts and unmatched source rows.

    03 / REVIEW

    Keep the human decision

    Inspect the evidence, record review notes and export the worklist. A finding is not a payment instruction, and opening it does not resolve it.

    !
    Current evaluation boundary

    This is a static browser-based pilot, not production accounting software. It has no server upload, user accounts, saved cloud history, ERP integrations, payment actions, currency conversion, tax/credit-note treatment or robust split-line matching. Use synthetic or approved anonymized data and validate outputs against original documents.
