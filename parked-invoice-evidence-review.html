01 / WHAT IS DELIVERED

A review package, not an anomaly score.

The buyer supplies one coherent set of invoice, purchase-order and goods-receipt CSV exports. The workbench compares supported fields and returns a reviewable record of what matched, what differed, what could not be established and where each value came from.

DELIVERABLE 01

Prioritised exception register

CSV worklist of candidate duplicate lines, missing references, supplier conflicts, price differences, quantity differences and receipt gaps supported by the supplied records.

DELIVERABLE 02

Source-linked evidence report

For each finding: the compared values, triggered rule, source file and row references, plus the related PO and receipt evidence where available.

DELIVERABLE 03

Unresolved-record register

Ambiguous matches, missing fields, incompatible suppliers, mixed or missing currencies and other cases where the available data cannot support a conclusion.

DELIVERABLE 04

Reviewer handoff

A prioritised list for the finance reviewer, with review notes and an explicit boundary between observed facts, candidate exceptions and confirmed outcomes.

DELIVERABLE 05

Reproducible calculations

Price and quantity comparisons are calculated from the supplied fields. Unsupported amounts are withheld rather than estimated from incomplete records.

DELIVERABLE 06

Coverage and limitations

A record of processed rows, excluded or ambiguous rows, configured checks and the limits of the supplied dataset. A clean result is not a certification that every invoice is correct.

02 / THE WORKFLOW

From a parked queue to an evidence-backed decision.

The product is deliberately narrow. It does not replace an ERP, approve invoices, contact suppliers or post accounting entries.

01 / INPUT

Invoice export

Invoice number, supplier, PO reference, item, quantity, price and currency.

02 / COMMITMENT

Approved PO

Supplier, PO, item, ordered quantity, agreed price and currency.

03 / RECEIPT

Goods receipt

Receipt number, PO, supplier, item, received quantity and date.

04 / ANALYSIS

Compare & classify

Apply explicit matching rules, preserve source-row references and isolate uncertainty.

05 / HANDOFF

Review & decide

Export the worklist and evidence report for a finance professional to verify.

Current supported scope: CSV only; one coherent dataset at a time. The existing paid-scope document defines up to 500 invoice lines, 500 PO lines and 500 goods-receipt lines, subject to file-fit and agreed acceptance criteria. XLSX, PDF and ERP connectors are not represented as live capabilities.

05 / PRODUCT BOUNDARY

What it does. What it does not claim.

The current product is a browser-based CSV workbench, not a multi-tenant enterprise SaaS or a managed recovery audit firm.

SUPPORTED

Invoice / PO / receipt checks

Source-linked line comparisons, configured exception rules, browser-side CSV processing and CSV/PDF-style evidence exports.

LIMITED

Payment and credit evidence

The separate recovery workbench has explicit calculation limits. Payment-excess candidates are not confirmed overpayments; statement-to-ledger reconciliation is not live.

NOT AVAILABLE

Enterprise operations

No ERP integration, multi-user tenancy, automated supplier claims, payment blocking, accounting write-back, independent security certification or verified bank settlement.

06 / MARKET ALTERNATIVES

We complement the finance system. We do not pretend to replace it.

Established providers already offer AP recovery audit, invoice control and supplier-statement reconciliation. LedgerTrace's intended entry point is a portable, source-linked second-pass review for a defined parked queue—not a claim to outperform enterprise platforms.
